Jane and Tarzan

Jane is a remote attestation server. It collects claims from elements through their trust agents, checks them against expected values with rules, and records the results, grouped into sessions.

Tarzan is the matching trust agent: a single binary that runs on an element and provides evidence such as TPM 2.0 quotes and PCRs, UEFI event logs, IMA logs and system information.
